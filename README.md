### Kajiado County Revenue Compliance & Field Operations

**Technical Director – Operations & Delivery | Sary Network International Ltd | Settle360**  
**April 2026 – Present · Kajiado County, Kenya**

As Technical Director, I am accountable for operational execution, delivery control and performance across a county revenue-compliance assignment.

I direct field execution, translate county priorities into operating plans and deployment decisions, lead field teams, oversee business verification and permit-related follow-up, and own operational reporting, data integrity and reconciliation. I report directly to the County Director General for Revenue and drive delivery across Revenue, Corporates and Business Permit (SBP) functions, engaging sub-county leadership, county billers and county security personnel.

The assignment includes legal and regulatory coordination with county officials and legal counsel, operational issue resolution, back-checks, exception management and controls designed to deter falsified records, irregular arrears adjustments, bribery and revenue leakage.

**Assignment-level outcomes:** **11,000+ businesses visited and verified**, **KSh 30M+ in invoices raised** and **KSh 14M+ in actual cash collected**. These are results recorded across the assignment, not personal cash-handling totals.

## How I approach operating problems

I start with the actual workflow: what needs to happen, who owns each step, where decisions or handoffs stall, what can go wrong and what information is needed to manage the work.

From there, I focus on practical execution: clear ownership, realistic targets, visible exceptions, reliable reporting and controls that teams can use. Technology is useful when it makes the operating model clearer, faster or more accountable.
