## How I approach problems

I usually start with the workflow rather than the tool.

I want to understand what people are actually doing, where work is getting stuck, who owns each step and what information is missing. From there, I look at what can be simplified, measured or automated.

That approach has carried across my operations roles, product work, ventures and technical projects.
