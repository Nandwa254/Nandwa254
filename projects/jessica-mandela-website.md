# Jessica Mandela — Artist Website

A client website built for gospel artist Jessica Mandela to bring her music, podcasts, videos, biography and booking enquiries into one place.

## The brief

The site needed to give the artist a stronger online presence while making it easy for visitors to discover her work, listen to music, view content and get in touch for professional enquiries.

## What was built

- artist-focused homepage and visual identity;
- music and listening-platform sections;
- podcast and video sections;
- biography and gallery;
- contact and booking forms;
- responsive layouts for desktop and mobile;
- email delivery for website enquiries;
- SEO and search indexing setup.

## My role

I handled the website from setup through delivery, including:

- UX and page structure;
- visual design and Elementor implementation;
- responsive layout;
- media and third-party integrations;
- contact and booking workflow;
- SEO configuration;
- SMTP / email setup;
- hosting and technical configuration;
- testing and troubleshooting.

## Technology

WordPress · Astra · Elementor · HTML / CSS · WPForms · WP Mail SMTP · Rank Math SEO · Popup Maker · Google Search Console

## Result

A complete public-facing website that brings the artist's music, media, profile and professional enquiries into one experience.
