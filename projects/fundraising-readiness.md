# HomeLink360 — Fundraising Readiness & Capital Planning

**Venture:** HomeLink360  
**Workstream:** Founder-led fundraising readiness and business planning  
**Stage:** Pre-seed planning

This work connects HomeLink360's customer proposition, early operating evidence, product sequence, operating controls and capital requirements into a coherent fundraising case.

## The question

An early-stage business needs to explain the customer problem, its service model, what evidence exists, what remains unproven and what the next stage of capital would fund. The story must be credible without presenting early signals as mature performance.

## Evidence base

The internal portfolio snapshot reviewed for planning contained:

- **65 transaction records**;
- **19 identifiable customers**;
- **KSh 721,850** in recorded principal;
- **KSh 149,660** in recorded service fees.

This was treated as early operating evidence, not a mature portfolio or statistically established product-market fit. Housing-linked support was kept distinct from later standalone salary-advance products.

## Product and operating story

The customer sequence is built around:

1. identifying a housing need;
2. search, viewing or placement;
3. moving and housing-related support;
4. appropriate follow-on financial services where suitable;
5. repayment history and customer relationship development.

The sequence is intended to remain consistent across product design, customer communication, operating workflows, records and investor materials.

## My role

I prepared the fundraising narrative, operating plan, product roadmap, customer model and use-of-funds plan. I also worked to keep the claims aligned with the evidence actually available and to make clear which parts of the model still required further validation.

## Funding plan

The planning materials described a proposed **US$75,000** raise for customer support, operations and compliance, platform automation and growth.

This was a funding target and use-of-funds plan, not a claim that the capital had already been raised.

## Diligence and limitations

The early evidence provides an operating signal, but it does not establish a mature portfolio, full regulatory readiness or proven scale economics. Further reconciliation, stronger operating controls and legal and regulatory work remain part of the venture's development.

## Status and public boundary

This case study describes pre-seed preparation and capital planning. Sensitive customer records, detailed financial statements and confidential diligence materials are not published.
