# HomeLink360 — Website & Digital Platform

**Live website:** https://homelink360.co.ke/

The HomeLink360 website is the public-facing digital layer of the venture, bringing together property listings, housing services, moving support and customer access.

## What was built

- public-facing venture website;
- property and housing listings;
- housing service pages;
- moving and support services;
- customer account and access flows;
- contact and enquiry paths;
- responsive layouts across desktop and mobile.

## My role

I handled the website setup, page structure, visual implementation, integrations, configuration, testing and ongoing improvements.

## Technology used

**WordPress · Astra · Elementor · WPForms · WP Mail SMTP · Rank Math SEO · Popup Maker · HTML / CSS**

The property and listing functionality is supported through the site's real-estate platform layer.
