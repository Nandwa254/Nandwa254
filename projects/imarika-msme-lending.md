# Imarika — Unsecured MSME Lending Product

**Role:** MSME Product Lead – Unsecured Lending  
**Organisation:** Choice Bank / Bees Credit Kenya  
**Period:** November 2024 – August 2025  
**Location:** Kenya

Imarika was an unsecured MSME lending product developed around the needs and operating realities of small businesses. My role covered product research, design and pilot execution, connecting field intelligence, credit requirements, customer journeys and frontline operations.

## Product leadership

I led product research, design and pilot execution, translating business and customer findings into customer journeys, lending processes and frontline priorities. The work required coordination across Sales, Credit, Compliance and Collections so that the proposition could be delivered through workable operating processes.

## Field research

I mapped and collected information from **400+ businesses across three counties**. The fieldwork helped establish a qualified business pipeline and gave the product team a practical view of customer needs and business operating conditions.

## Credit-scoring methodology and system

I designed and implemented the credit-scoring methodology and supporting system for the unsecured MSME lending product. The work established a structured and repeatable approach to assessing applications and supporting credit decisions.

Detailed scoring parameters, decision rules and internal implementation are not published because they formed part of a live lending operation.

## Product and operating workflows

The product work included:

- customer and market research;
- lending proposition and product structure;
- customer onboarding and journey design;
- credit-assessment methodology and support system;
- coordination of sales, credit, compliance and collections;
- loan tracking and operational visibility;
- pilot execution and feedback.

A custom loan-tracking platform supported operations and finance with clearer loan status, repayment monitoring and collaboration across the teams involved in delivery.

## Related operational systems

The Imarika product work sat alongside a separate set of operational systems developed during my overlapping Operations Manager appointment at Bees Credit Kenya. These included loan-stage time measurement and a branch issue escalation and resolution workflow.

The work brought together SME field research, unsecured lending product development, credit-scoring design, workflow development and cross-functional execution. It demonstrates product ownership grounded in field evidence and operational delivery.
