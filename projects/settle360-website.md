# Settle360 — Website & Digital Platform

**Live website:** https://settle360.co.ke/

The Settle360 website is the public-facing digital platform for the business support and outsourcing venture.

## What was built

- company website and service presentation;
- customer-facing information structure;
- service and solution pages;
- contact and enquiry paths;
- responsive layouts across desktop and mobile;
- supporting property/listing functionality.

## My role

I handled the website setup, page structure, visual implementation, integrations, configuration, testing and ongoing improvements.

## Technology used

**WordPress · Astra · Elementor · WPForms · WP Mail SMTP · Rank Math SEO · Popup Maker · HTML / CSS**

The site also uses a real-estate/listing platform layer for the property-related functionality.
