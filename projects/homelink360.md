# HomeLink360 — Housing-Led Services & Financial Services Venture

**Role:** Head of Growth  
**Period:** August 2025 – Present  
**Location:** Kenya  
**Website:** https://homelink360.co.ke/

HomeLink360 is a housing-led service and financial-services venture built around the practical needs people face when finding, moving into and settling in a home. The proposition begins with housing-related services and customer access, with the potential to support appropriate additional financial-service pathways as the relationship develops.

## Customer proposition

The venture is designed around the move-and-settle journey rather than beginning with a generic lending product. Its current public service offering includes:

- house tours and property-viewing support;
- moving and transport services;
- support with transport-related fees;
- housing-related deposit-charge support;
- home and office cleaning.

The service model is being refined as the venture develops its customer journeys, operating workflows and digital access.

## My role

As Head of Growth, I work on the venture's market positioning and commercial development. My responsibilities include:

- refining the housing-led service framework and proposition;
- identifying customer segments and opportunities to improve the offer;
- translating customer and market needs into customer journeys and service workflows;
- setting priorities for digital product development and customer access;
- coordinating the development of digital platforms and supporting workflows;
- using customer learning and early operating evidence to refine the model.

## Product and operating model

The intended customer journey begins with a housing need, then supports search, tours, placement and move-related services. The wider relationship can create a basis for appropriate housing-linked support and other financial services, subject to the relevant product design, customer suitability and regulatory requirements.

The principle is to build around a real customer need and a useful service relationship, rather than lead with finance before the housing need is understood.

## Early operating evidence

An internal portfolio snapshot previously reviewed for venture planning contained **65 transaction records across 19 identifiable customers**, with **KSh 721,850 in recorded principal** and **KSh 149,660 in service fees**.

This is early-stage operating evidence, not a mature portfolio-performance claim. The figures should not be interpreted as independently audited financial statements or as proof of product-market fit.
