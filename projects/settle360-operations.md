# Settle360 — Business Support, Outsourcing & Field Delivery

**Venture:** Settle360  
**Role:** Founder & Operations Lead  
**Period:** 2025 – Present  
**Website:** https://settle360.co.ke/

Settle360 is a business-support and outsourcing venture focused on translating client requirements into organised execution. Its operating model brings together team deployment, field activity, verification, reporting, reconciliation, quality checks and delivery controls.

## The operating proposition

Many client assignments depend on work happening consistently beyond the office: people must be recruited or deployed, responsibilities made clear, activity verified, exceptions resolved and results reported in a form that supports decisions.

Settle360 is built around that delivery requirement. The emphasis is not simply supplying people or completing visits. It is establishing an operating structure that connects the assignment brief to field execution and a reliable close-out process.

## My role

As Founder & Operations Lead, I shape the venture's service proposition and operating model and lead delivery routines across client assignments. This includes:

- translating client requirements into delivery plans and operating procedures;
- organising team recruitment, onboarding, deployment and supervision;
- setting responsibilities, targets, reporting routines and escalation paths;
- overseeing field verification, back-checks and quality checks;
- maintaining exception handling, reconciliation and reporting;
- resolving delivery bottlenecks and improving the operating approach.

## Kajiado County revenue-compliance assignment

The Kajiado County revenue-compliance engagement is a major example of field delivery at scale.

**Assignment role:** Technical Director – Operations & Delivery  
**Delivery arrangement:** Settle360 and Sary Network International Ltd jointly undertook the county contract as delivery partners. I held the Technical Director designation within this engagement.  
**Period:** April 2026 – Present

The assignment involves operational execution, a 13-person field team, business engagement and verification, permit-related follow-up, coordination with county revenue functions and sub-county leadership, performance reporting, reconciliation and field-integrity controls.

**Assignment-level outcomes:**
- **11,000+ businesses visited and verified**
- **KSh 30M+ in invoices raised**
- **KSh 14M+ in actual cash collected**

These are results recorded across the assignment and delivered through the wider field operation. They are not presented as personal cash-handling totals.
